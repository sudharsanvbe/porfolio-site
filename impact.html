Re-Spectable

Founder · In partnership with VHS Chennai
"Turn your Hindsight into Someone else's Foresight."

At five, a pair of glasses corrected an undiagnosed astigmatism and changed how Krshay saw the world — that clarity later shaped a student-athlete and league cricketer.

Re-Spectable is an India-based online database connecting high-quality used glasses with those who need them most, using lensometer verification and a QR-code ledger for clinical accuracy and offline field sorting.

1M1B AppliedAI

Curriculum Designer & Pilot Facilitator

Designed a 3-tier AI curriculum for 1M1B innovation labs across Northeast India, where only 18.5% of rural schools have internet access and 280M workers face AI-driven displacement by 2030 (McKinsey, 2023). The curriculum runs entirely in-browser using Indian datasets and embedded LLM prompts.

"Finally, a session which has helped and will be applicable in life." — Student, Young Scholar's Academy

Piloted over two days (Jun 16–17, 2026) at Young Scholar's Academy, Bangalore, with 55 Grade 8–9 students building image-classification models to solve real community problems.
