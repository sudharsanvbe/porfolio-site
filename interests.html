What travel means, so far

Travel has mostly happened through sport — a passport that fills up one start line at a time. Bahrain was the first stamp from competing rather than sightseeing, and it changed how travel is thought of: not a break from training, but an extension of it.

Every Nationals trip adds a city, a new track, a new crowd — comparing notes with competitors from other states.
