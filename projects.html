01
Executive Summary

Measuring recovery through movement.

An IMU-based embedded system using an ESP32 to quantify ACL rehabilitation recovery through objective, data-driven movement tracking.

02
The Problem

From subjective assessment to data.

Traditional ACL recovery tracking depends heavily on periodic clinical evaluation and can miss important daily movement deviations.

03
Research Objective

Continuous motion intelligence.

Transform wearable sensor information into measurable indicators that help monitor joint movement and rehabilitation progress.

01
Engineering Solution
01
Sensor Fusion Accelerometer and gyroscope data for joint movement.
02
Edge Processing ESP32-based filtering and embedded processing.
03
Human-Centered Design Wearable compression sleeve designed for movement.
02
System Architecture

Wearable → Processing → Data

Sensor measurements are collected through the wearable system, processed on the ESP32, and converted into useful movement information.
